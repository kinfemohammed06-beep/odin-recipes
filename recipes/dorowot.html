Doro Wot Recipe

Home

Description

Doro wot is a stew made from chicken and eggs, mainly. It contains myriad of spices which makes the stew bearable to only a few. The butter added only after the stew boili up very good and the chicken tenders up is alio another ingredient. Doro wot is one of the main dish of Ethiopia. It is regarded by the Ethiopians with reverence and only served in many of Ethiopian Households as a Holiday recipe.

ingredients

  • Chicken
  • eggs
  • tomato
  • onion
  • Ginger and Garlic
  • oil
  • korarima(Green cardamom)
  • pepper
  • nutmeg
  • berbere(red hot pepper)
  • netter kibe(Butter)
  • injera

Steps

  1. Prepare your ingredients
  2. Prepare the Ginger-Garlic Paste
  3. Caramelize the Onions
  4. Add the reserved ginger-garlic paste and the 2 tablespoons of tomato paste into the pot with the onions. Sauté for 2 to 3 minutes,
  5. Add the 1/2 cup of berbere spice blend and stir well to combine. Sauté for about 1 minute, then pour in 2 cups of water. Increase the heat to medium, bring the mixture to a low simmer, and let it cook for about 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in the 1/3 cup of niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter), 1 teaspoon korarima (or green cardamom), 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg, and salt. Allow the sauce to simmer gently for another 5 minutes.
  7. Add the bone-in chicken thighs to the pot. Reduce the heat to low, cover with a lid, and let it braise until the chicken is very tender—about 1 hour.
  8. Gently nestle the peeled hard-boiled eggs into the stew and simmer for about 10 more minutes so they absorb the flavors. Adjust the salt or add a little extra water if you prefer a thinner sauce, then serve hot with injera.
